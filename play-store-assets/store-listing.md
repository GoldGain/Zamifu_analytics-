## Full description

Zamifu Analytics is a school management platform designed for Kenyan schools. Bring learner records, assessments, report cards, fees, timetables, communication, and academic insights together in one secure workspace.

With Zamifu Analytics, authorized school teams can:

• Manage learner profiles, admission details, class enrolment, and academic history.
• Upload results and generate report cards with support for CBE and 8-4-4 workflows.
• Track fee invoices, payments, balances, and receipts.
• Organize classes, teacher assignments, timetables, and exam schedules.
• Share announcements and reminders with the school community.
• Review analytics for attendance, fee collection, and academic performance.
• Give teachers, students, parents, school administrators, and platform administrators role-appropriate access.
• Use Pathway Finder to explore senior-school pathway interests across STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts and Sports Science.
• Access Zamifu Copilot and other assisted features where enabled by the school workspace.

The app is built for schools that want clearer records, faster reporting, and better communication between educators, learners, and parents. Schools are responsible for entering accurate information and obtaining any permissions required for learner data, including data relating to children.

Zamifu Analytics does not replace professional judgment or official academic decisions. AI-assisted comments and pathway recommendations should be reviewed by qualified staff before use.

Download Zamifu Analytics and connect your school community with smarter, simpler school operations.
