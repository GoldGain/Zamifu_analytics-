# Islamic Religious Education — Kenya Junior School Grades 7, 8 and 9

## Verified result

The official Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) Grade Seven, Grade Eight and Grade Nine curriculum-design landing pages were checked first. Each landing page exposes an **IRE** embedded Google Drive preview. The independent, accessible Easylearn mirror describes its files as revised copies from KICD and provides direct PDF downloads for all three grades. The downloaded PDFs have KICD title pages, identify the subject as *Islamic Religious Education*, and state the relevant grade and 2024 revision/publication information. The exact hierarchy below is transcribed from each PDF's numbered **SUMMARY OF STRANDS AND SUB-STRANDS** table, then checked against the numbered strand tables in the body of the same PDF. [1] [2] [3] [7] [8] [9] [10]

All three grades are verified from accessible KICD-labelled mirror PDFs. The official landing-page links themselves were verified, but the Google Drive files returned “Sorry, the owner hasn't given you permission to download this file” when direct download was attempted. Therefore, the local PDFs are the accessible mirror copies, not bytes downloaded from KICD's Google Drive. This is the principal limitation on official-PDF byte-level verification.

## Cross-check and disagreements resolved

The independent mirror source is Easylearn's **Curriculum Design** page, which states that its Junior School curriculum-design files are revised copies from KICD and links direct PDFs for Grade 7, Grade 8 and Grade 9 IRE. [7] The direct PDFs were downloaded and processed with `pdftotext -layout`; their title pages identify KICD and the relevant grade. The official KICD landing pages separately expose an IRE iframe for each grade: Grade 7 Drive preview ID `1Po_NF_oEsMMYXukVcM82nVEqXS7yY2Zu`, Grade 8 ID `10HXITblf3E9bwYFABlcbv_qA9ZGSSod1`, and Grade 9 ID `1QAtDW4Pq0enTVPOb32TlfQzge2au4RRC`. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

No strand or sub-strand disagreement was found within any downloaded grade PDF: the numbered summary table and the corresponding numbered body tables agree on the grade-specific hierarchy. The only apparent wording differences are the source's own typography choices: `Qur’an` in the summary versus `QURAN` in some body section headers for Grades 7 and 9, and the literal Grade 9 space in `Ulum al- Qur’an`. These were not normalized. Grade-specific differences were retained rather than inferred as progression: for example, Grade 7 has `1.1 Ulumul Qur’an`, Grade 8 has `1.1 Modes of preservation of the Qur’an`, and Grade 9 has `1.1 Ulum al- Qur’an`; Grade 7's Muamalat has three sub-strands, Grade 8's has four, and Grade 9's has six.

## Limitations

1. KICD's three landing pages were accessible as HTML and their IRE Google Drive preview links were extracted. The Google Drive `uc?export=download` endpoint refused all three files because the owner disabled downloading. The official preview URLs are therefore recorded as source evidence, but no claim is made that the local mirror bytes are an official KICD-hosted download.
2. The accessible mirror copies are KICD-labelled and are used for exact text extraction and page-level verification. They are not silently treated as the KICD-hosted originals.
3. Counts are counts of numbered strands and numbered sub-strands in each grade's summary table. Suggested lesson totals are reported separately because they are not hierarchy counts.
