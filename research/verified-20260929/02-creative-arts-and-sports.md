## Disagreements and limitations

The summary tables are internally inconsistent with some later body headings. To avoid inventing or silently normalising data, the report uses the explicitly numbered **SUMMARY OF STRAND AND SUB STRAND** table as the hierarchy source and records the discrepancies here:

- **Grade 7:** the summary table lists `2.8 Kenyan Folk song` and `2.9 Swimming (Optional)`. Later body headings reverse those two numbers: the swimming section is headed `2.8 Swimming`, while the folk-song section is headed `2.9 Kenyan Folk songs`.
- **Grade 8:** the summary table lists `2.8 Kenyan Folk Dance` and `2.9 Swimming (Optional)`. Later body headings reverse those two numbers: the swimming section is headed `2.8 Swimming`, while the folk-dance section is headed `2.9 Kenyan Folk Dance`.
- **Grade 9:** the summary table lists `2.11.1 Swimming (Optional)` and `2.12.2 Kenyan Indigenous Games (Optional)`. The later optional-section body headings use `2.11.1 Swimming` and `2.11.2 Kenyan Indigenous Games`. The independent Doyen Grade 9 copy reproduces the summary table's `2.12.2` numbering, so the summary-table transcription above is retained.

No progression was inferred, no grades were merged, and no generic subject pack was reused. The official KICD landing pages were accessible as HTML but their embedded Google Drive files could not be downloaded because the owner/editor download restriction was returned. Therefore the exact names are verified from the complete, grade-specific, KICD-labelled Arena PDFs, with Grade 9 additionally cross-checked against the Doyen PDF. If a future official download becomes available, the body/table numbering discrepancies should be rechecked against that official file.
