# Agriculture and Nutrition: KICD Junior School curriculum, Grades 7–9

The current KICD-linked 2024 designs provide separate, complete numbered hierarchies for each grade. The source PDFs were identified as the proofread Grade 7, Grade 8 and Grade 9 Agriculture designs; the summary hierarchy appears on PDF page 12 in each file. The entries below retain the source wording and spelling. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

The teacher.co.ke Grade 7 and Grade 8 copies are older drafts and disagree materially with the current KICD-linked designs. A Grade 9 CBCelimu copy broadly agrees but differs in some wording and spelling. Those secondary copies were used only as cross-checks; the current KICD-linked viewer controls. [7] [8] [9] [10]

## Verified hierarchy

### Grade 7 — 4 strands, 14 sub-strands

**1.0 Conservation of Resources**
- 1.1 Controlling Soil Pollution
- 1.2 Constructing Water Retention Structures
- 1.3 Conserving Food Nutrients
- 1.4 Growing Trees

**2.0 Food Production Processes**
- 2.1 Crop Establishment
- 2.2 Selected Crop Management Practises
- 2.3 Preparing Animal Products
- 2.4 Cooking Food

**3.0 Hygiene Practises**
- 3.1 Hygiene in Rearing Animals
- 3.2 Laundry: Loose-coloured Items

**4.0 Production Techniques**
- 4.1 Knitting Skills
- 4.2 Constructing Framed Suspended Garden
- 4.3 Adding Value to Crop Produce
- 4.4 Making Homemade Soap

### Grade 8 — 4 strands, 12 sub-strands

**1.0 Conservation of Resources**
- 1.1 Soil Conservation Measures
- 1.2 Water Harvesting and Storage

**2.0 Food Production Processes**
- 2.1 Kitchen and Backyard Gardening
- 2.2 Poultry Rearing in a Fold
- 2.3 Crop Pest and Disease Control
- 2.4 Preparation of Animal Products
- 2.5 Preserving Animal Products
- 2.6 Cooking: Preparing a Balanced Meal

**3.0 Hygiene Practices**
- 3.1 Cleaning the Kitchen

**4.0 Production Techniques**
- 4.1 Sewing Skills: Constructing Household Items
- 4.2 Constructing Innovative Animal Waterer
- 4.3 ICT Support Services

### Grade 9 — 4 strands, 10 sub-strands

**1.0 Conservation of Resources**
- 1.1 Conserving Animal Feed: Hay
- 1.2 Conserving Leftover Food
- 1.3 Integrated Farming

**2.0 Food Production Processes**
- 2.1 Organic Gardening
- 2.2 Storage of Crop Produce
- 2.3 Cooking: Using Flour Mixtures

**3.0 Hygiene Practises**
- 3.1 Cleaning Waste Disposal Facilities
- 3.2 Disinfecting Clothing and Household Articles

**4.0 Production Techniques**
- 4.1 Grafting in Plants
- 4.2 Homemade Sun Dryer
