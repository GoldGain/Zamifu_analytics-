# Kiswahili: KICD Junior School curriculum, Grades 7–9

The current KICD-linked public previews identify the revised October 2024 Grade 7, Grade 8 and Grade 9 designs. The official summary pages are titled “Muhtasari wa Mada na Mada Ndogo.” Their four learning-skill strands are retained as the first level, and the 60 numbered Mada Ndogo rows in each grade are the terminal sub-strands. The broader theme headings are not added as a third tier. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

The older Grade 7 and Grade 8 mirror extracts were excluded. Current CBCelimu pages were found for all three grades, but their page text could not be retrieved for independent row-by-row verification; they are leads, not hierarchy evidence. The exact names below therefore follow the current KICD-linked previews. [7] [8] [9]
