### Grade 7 internal discrepancy

The Grade 7 PDF has an internal pagination/appendix inconsistency. Its summary (PDF p. 12) and detailed curriculum pages (PDF pp. 13, 15, 17, 20, 22, 25, 27, 30 and 33) consistently give four strands and the nine entries above. However, the table of contents omits the `4.0 Force and Energy` line, and Appendix 2 labels the electrical/magnetism entries as `4.2 Electrical Energy` and `4.3 Magnetism`; the appendix also expands the Grade 7 `3.2 Human Excretory System` entry to “Human Excretory System-Skin and Kidneys.” These appendix-only variants were not substituted into the requested hierarchy because the summary and the actual detailed curriculum pages agree with one another and provide the numbered strand/sub-strand structure. The discrepancy is recorded rather than silently corrected.

## Limitations and resolution

1. The official KICD landing pages were reachable and their Integrated Science iframe IDs were recovered from the page HTML. Direct Google Drive download was not permitted by the file owner, so the official binary PDFs could not be saved locally. This is why the local evidence paths are accessible KICD-labelled mirrors rather than a claim that the official binaries were downloaded.
2. All grades were still verifiable because each independent PDF is directly downloadable, identifies KICD, and contains the full summary and detailed curriculum pages. No grade required an empty entry or an `incomplete` status.
3. The files use printed page numbers that differ from PDF page indices. Both are reported above. Hierarchy names and counts come from the summary pages, not inferred progression across grades.
4. Grade 7’s appendix and table of contents conflict with its summary and detailed pages as described above. The summary/detailed-page agreement was used to resolve the conflict, and the alternative appendix labels were retained as a limitation rather than merged into the answer.
