## Limitations and disagreements resolved

1. The official KICD landing pages expose the Mathematics files through Google Drive preview iframes. The official previews were accessible and were used to verify title, grade, revised/publication information, ISBN and total page count. However, direct `uc?export=download` requests returned Google Drive's message that the owner had not granted download permission. Therefore the locally saved PDFs are the direct Easylearn mirror copies, not a fabricated or renamed official download. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]
2. The local PDFs are encrypted with copy/print restrictions reported by `pdfinfo`, but `pdftotext -layout` extracted the hierarchy successfully. No OCR was needed.
3. In the Grade 8 summary-table extraction, the row for the second Measurements sub-strand contained an apparent `L Area` artefact. The body heading and numbered row clearly read **3.2 Area** on printed p. 21, so the verified name is **3.2 Area**, not the extraction artefact. [9]
4. Grade 8 uses lower-case `graphs` in the numbered summary/body row for **4.2 Coordinates and graphs**; the display heading capitalises `Graphs`. The numbered row is retained exactly as **4.2 Coordinates and graphs**. [9]
5. Grade 7's summary row visually carries `Data Handling and Probability` without the `5.0` prefix because of the table layout, while the table of contents and section heading explicitly identify **STRAND 5.0: DATA HANDLING AND PROBABILITY**. The numbered strand is therefore recorded as **5.0 Data Handling and Probability**. [8]
6. No progression, cross-grade merging, generic pack, or inferred strand was used. Each grade was transcribed from its own grade-specific current PDF and counted independently.
