## Verified hierarchy

The viewer identifies the files as KICD Junior School Curriculum Designs and states that they were published and printed by the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development.[4] [5] [6]

The numbered hierarchy below is transcribed from the `SUMMARY OF STRANDS AND SUB STRANDS` page of the accessible mirror PDFs. The summary table is used as the controlling hierarchy because it is the document's numbered index; any different wording in a detailed section heading is recorded under **Disagreements resolved** rather than silently merged.

## Disagreements resolved and limitations

1. **Official download restriction.** Direct Google Drive download attempts for all three KICD-embedded files returned Google Drive's permission message that only the owner and editors can download. The files remained viewable in the official viewers, which expose the KICD title, grade, revision information, ISBN and “Published and printed by the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development” statement.[4] [5] [6] Therefore, the local PDFs are accessible education-resource mirrors, not claimed to be direct official downloads.
2. **Grade 7 table of contents versus summary numbering.** The Grade 7 table of contents prints `STRAND 2:`, `STRAND 3:`, `STRAND 4:` and `STRAND 5:` without `.0`, while its numbered summary rows and detailed table rows use `2.0`, `3.0`, `4.0` and `5.0`. The hierarchy above follows the numbered summary rows and records the TOC variant rather than inventing a new numbering scheme.[7, pp. vi, xii]
3. **Grade 8 sub-strand 3.2.** The Grade 8 summary table says `3.2 Ceramics` on printed page x, while the detailed section heading says `3.2 Ceramic Materials` on printed page 13 (local PDF page 24). The JSON/report hierarchy uses the summary-table wording `3.2 Ceramics`; the detailed-section wording is preserved here as a documented disagreement.[8, pp. x and 13]
4. **Mirror formatting differences.** The independent Grade 7 Doyen copy uses variants such as `ICT tools in Communication`, `computer hardware` and `Freehand sketching` in places where the Teachers Palace copy's summary table uses `ICT Tools in Communication`, `Computer Hardware` and `Free-hand sketching`. These are capitalization/spacing differences between mirror renderings, not merged or inferred progression. The primary strings above remain the exact summary-table strings from the cited extraction copy.[7] [10]

No progression, merged grade, generic pack or inferred subject data was used. No database, application file, credential or protected system was modified.
