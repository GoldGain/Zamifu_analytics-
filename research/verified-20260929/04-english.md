# English for Kenya Junior School Grades 7, 8 and 9 — verified hierarchy

All three grades are **complete** at the numbered-theme/sub-strand level. The official KICD landing pages were checked first. Each page identifies an English iframe whose Google Drive preview URL is recorded below. Direct Drive download was blocked by the owner setting, so the local PDFs are accessible mirror copies of the KICD-labelled designs, downloaded and checked with `pdfinfo` and `pdftotext -layout`.

The JSON/report structure treats each numbered `THEME` as one top-level `strand` because that is the numbered hierarchy used in the English designs. Each theme has five numbered sub-strands: Listening and Speaking (`x.1.1`), Reading 1 (`x.2.1`), Grammar (`x.3.1`), Reading 2 (`x.4.1`), and Writing (`x.5.1`). The unnumbered domain labels are not merged into or substituted for the numbered themes.

## Disagreements and limitations

- The KICD landing pages expose the official PDFs through Google Drive preview iframes. The direct-download endpoint returned an explicit owner permission error for all three IDs. The official page URLs and preview URLs are nevertheless recorded; no credentials or access workarounds were used.
- The revised mirror summaries sometimes use compact labels while detailed theme tables expand the same item (for example, Grade 7 summary `1.1.1 Oral skills –polite language` versus the first detailed table header `Conversational Skills: Polite Language`). To avoid silently rewriting the source, the JSON and hierarchy above use the **numbered SUMMARY OF STRANDS AND SUBSTRANDS wording**, preserving its punctuation and spelling; detailed tables were used to confirm the numbering and five-row-per-theme structure.
- Teacher.co.ke copies provide a second accessible education-resource mirror for Grades 7–8. Their detailed headings may expand or capitalize a summary label differently, but the theme sequence and numbered positions agree. The Arena Grade 9 PDF confirms the 15 numbered theme sequence and detailed table structure; its opening summary is an older unnumbered domain summary, so it was not substituted for the revised Grade 9 numbered summary.
- Grade 8 preserves the PDF spelling `RESPONSIBILITES` in `THEME 4: CONSUMER ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITES`. Grade 9 preserves `auxialiaries` in `3.3.1 Modal auxialiaries`, as printed in the revised summary. These are source spellings, not corrections.
- Counts are exact counts of the numbered hierarchy requested: 15 themes × 5 numbered sub-strands = 75 per grade. No learning outcomes, activities, progression claims, or inferred cross-grade mappings were added.
