# Islamic Religious Education (IRE), Kenyan Junior School Grades 7–9

## Scope and source discipline
This note records only strands and sub-strands visible in curriculum-design PDF text obtained during this research. KICD is the authoritative publisher. The KICD curriculum-design index and grade pages were checked, but the current web extractor returned only the site footer, so direct page-level links could not be harvested reliably. A direct KICD PDF URL was successfully identified for Grade 8; Grade 7 and Grade 9 PDFs were cross-checked through secondary hosts because the KICD page links were not exposed by the extractor. Secondary copies are treated as locating/cross-checking aids, not as independent authority.

## Verified grade-specific structures
### Grade 7
The extracted Grade 7 contents and section tables verify these strand names: Qur’an; Hadith; Pillars of Iman; Devotional Acts; Akhlaq (Moral Values); Muamalat (Social Relationship); Islamic Heritage and Civilisation. Verified sub-strand labels from the section tables are: Qur’an—Ulumul Qur’an; Selected [chapter/topic text is split in extraction and should be checked against the page image before display]. Hadith—Ulumul Hadith; Selected [Hadith title is split in extraction]. Pillars of Iman—Significance of Tawheed; Shirk. Devotional Acts—Swalah; Zakat; Saum. Muamalat—three sub-strands are present, but the first and third names are line-wrapped/truncated in the available text extraction; the second is Trade. Islamic Heritage and Civilisation—Reforms [introduced by Prophet Muhammad (s.a.w.)]. Akhlaq has three sub-strands, but the extracted table text line-wraps their names and does not support safe verbatim reconstruction. These uncertain labels are intentionally not promoted into the JSON arrays.

### Grade 8
The KICD PDF summary table verifies: Qur’an—Ulum al-Qur’an; Selected Chapter Surah Al-Hujurat (Q 49). Hadith—Ulum al-Hadith; Selected Hadith. Pillars of Iman—Belief in the Last Day (Day of Judgement); Belief in Qadar. Devotional Acts—Shariah (Islamic law); Tawbah (Repentance). Akhlaq—Virtues in Islam; Significance of Islamic Morality; Prohibitions in Islam. Muamalat—Domestic violence; Iddah; Child custody; Polygamy in Islam; Trade and Finance in Islam; Contemporary Issues. Islamic Heritage and Civilisation—Islam in Kenya; Unity of Muslims; Muslim Institutions.

### Grade 9
A Grade 9 PDF is located and its contents identify the same seven high-level strand families (Qur’an, Hadith, Pillars of Iman, Devotional Acts, Akhlaq, Muamalat, and Islamic Heritage and Civilisation), but the available text extraction in this run did not yield a complete, reliable grade-specific summary table of sub-strand names. To avoid inventing or silently borrowing Grade 8 labels, Grade 9 arrays should remain empty until the official Grade 9 KICD PDF is directly verified page-by-page.

## Implementation cautions
1. Do not infer Grade 7 or Grade 9 content by copying Grade 8: progression and rationalisation can change sub-strand names and scope.
2. Preserve spelling and parenthetical wording exactly as printed, including Qur’an/Quran, Tawbah, Iddah, and “Islamic Heritage and Civilisation.”
3. Treat “Selected Hadith” and similar labels as official only when the PDF table supports the exact title; do not expand a line-wrapped extraction by guesswork.
4. Keep arrays empty where a grade-specific item cannot be verified. Store the gap in notes and retain the source URL for later page-image verification.
5. The source PDFs contain suggested learning outcomes and experiences; this research note does not paraphrase them as official outcomes because the requested schema asks for strands/sub-strands and the extraction did not provide a safe, complete outcome transcription.
