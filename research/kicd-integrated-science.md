# Research note: Integrated Science — Kenyan Junior School Grades 7–9

## Scope and source method

This note records only curriculum structure that could be verified against KICD-hosted or KICD-indexed curriculum-design material. The authoritative landing pages are KICD's [Grade Seven Designs](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/grade-seven-designs/), [Grade Eight Designs](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/grade-eight-designs/), and [Grade Nine Designs](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/grade-nine-designs/). The PDF URLs identified in KICD search indexing are:

- Grade 7: https://kicd.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/INTEGRATED-SCIENCE-GRADE-7-DESIGN-AMENDED-JANUARY-2022.pdf
- Grade 8: https://kicd.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Integrated-Science-Grade-8-Curriculum-Design-1.pdf
- Grade 9: https://kicd.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/GRADE.9.INTEGRATED.SCIENCE.pdf

The KICD web pages rendered incompletely in text extraction, and direct PDF retrieval was unavailable in this sandbox (404 responses). Grade 7 and Grade 8 structures were therefore transcribed from accessible copies that identify themselves as KICD curriculum designs, and cross-checked against KICD's indexed titles/snippets. These copies are secondary access routes, not substitutes for the official URLs above.

## Verified grade-specific structure

### Grade 7

The accessible Grade 7 design's table of contents and strand headings show these strands and sub-strands:

| Strand | Sub-strands |
|---|---|
| Scientific Investigation | Introduction to Integrated Science; Laboratory Safety; Basic Science Process Skills; Laboratory Apparatus and Instruments |
| Mixtures | Mixtures; Acids, bases and indicators; Solid–Liquid Separation Techniques |
| Living Things and their Environment | Reproduction in Plants; Human Reproductive System |
| Force and Energy | Static Electricity; Current Electricity; Magnetism |

The exact KICD-hosted Grade 7 PDF URL is listed above. The accessible copy is the 2024 teacher.co.ke mirror of the Grade 7 design: https://teacher.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/GRADE-7-CURRICULUM-DESIGNS-INTEGRATED-SCIENCE-2024-TEACHER.CO_.KE_.pdf. Because the mirror can differ from an amended or rationalised release, implementations should re-check the live KICD file before treating wording or sequencing as current.

### Grade 9

## Implementation cautions

1. **Do not merge grade structures.** Grade 7's “Mixtures” is not the same strand label as Grade 8's “Mixtures, Elements and Compounds,” even where concepts develop progressively.
2. **Treat names as version-sensitive.** KICD has amended, draft, and rationalised design releases; store the exact PDF URL, retrieval date, and document title alongside imported curriculum data.
3. **Preserve official spelling and punctuation.** In particular, retain “sub-strand” labels as printed, including “Solid–Liquid Separation Techniques” and capitalisation of “Static Electricity.”
4. **Do not infer Grade 9 outcomes.** Until the official Grade 9 PDF is retrievable and inspected, leave grade-9 arrays empty and flag the gap rather than importing a publisher's table.
5. **Outcomes are not supplied by this note.** The task requested strand/sub-strand names; learning-outcome text must be copied only from the corresponding official design and not reconstructed from summaries.
6. **Verify implementation release.** Before production use, download the three KICD PDFs from the official links and compare page headings, version/date, strand numbering, and sub-strand wording with this note.
