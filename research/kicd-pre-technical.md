# Pre-Technical Studies: Kenyan Junior School Grades 7–9

## Conclusion

The Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) curriculum-design index pages list **Pre-Technical Studies** for Grades 7, 8, and 9. The official KICD PDF endpoints surfaced by search are currently unavailable to direct download in this environment, so the grade-specific names below were transcribed from readable PDF mirrors that identify the documents as KICD curriculum designs. The mirrors reproduce the KICD title pages, publication information, and curriculum tables. The KICD index pages remain the authoritative catalogue sources.

The Grade 7 and Grade 8 documents are revised 2024 designs. The Grade 9 revised 2024 mirror gives a coherent Grade 9 progression and is used below. A second Grade 9 mirror appears to contain an earlier or differently edited version; its conflicting entries are recorded explicitly rather than silently merged.

## Grade 7: verified strand and sub-strand names

The readable Grade 7 PDF is titled **Junior School Curriculum Design: Pre-Technical Studies, Grade 7** and identifies itself as a revised July 2024 design. Its summary table lists five strands and thirteen sub-strands:

| Strand | Sub-strands |
|---|---|
| **1.0 Foundations of Pre-Technical Studies** | 1.1 Introduction to Pre-Technical Studies; 1.2 Safety in the Immediate Environment; 1.3 Computer Concepts |
| **2.0 Communication in Pre-Technical Studies** | 2.1 Introduction to Drawing; 2.2 Free-hand sketching; 2.3 ICT Tools in Communication |
| **3.0 Materials for Production** | 3.1 Introduction to Materials; 3.2 Metallic Materials; 3.3 Non-Metallic Materials |
| **4.0 Tools and Production** | 4.1 Measuring and Marking Out Tools; 4.2 Computer Hardware |
| **5.0 Entrepreneurship** | 5.1 Introduction to Entrepreneurship; 5.2 Production Unit; 5.3 Financial Goals |

The source’s summary table states that the suggested total is 120 lessons and cautions that the number of lessons per sub-strand may vary by context. The names above are transcribed as names, not inferred from learning outcomes.

## Grade 8: verified strand and sub-strand names

The Grade 8 PDF is titled **Junior School Curriculum Design: Pre-Technical Studies, Grade 8**, states “First published 2023” and “Revised 2024,” and says it was published and printed by KICD. Its summary table lists five strands and twelve sub-strands:

| Strand | Sub-strands |
|---|---|
| **1.0 Foundations of Pre-Technical Studies** | 1.1 Fire Safety; 1.2 Data Safety |
| **2.0 Communication in Pre-Technical Studies** | 2.1 Plane Geometry; 2.2 Dimensioning; 2.3 Plain Scale Drawing; 2.4 Visual Programming |
| **3.0 Materials for Production** | 3.1 Composite Materials; 3.2 Ceramic Materials |
| **4.0 Tools and Production** | 4.1 Cutting Tools; 4.2 Computer Software |
| **5.0 Entrepreneurship** | 5.1 Bookkeeping; 5.2 Income and Budgeting; 5.3 Marketing goods and Services; 5.4 Distribution of Goods and Services |

The table of contents and summary table use “Ceramics” in the table of contents but “Ceramic Materials” in the detailed summary and sub-strand heading. For an application data model, use **Ceramic Materials** as the grade-specific sub-strand name because it is the detailed heading and summary-table entry; preserve the table-of-contents wording only as a source-text variant. The summary table gives 120 suggested lessons and says the allocation may be more or less depending on context.

### Grade 9 source conflict and implementation caution

A separate Grade 9 PDF mirror hosted by eEducation Group contains a different summary: it lists **1.2 Self-Exploration and Career Development**, **1.3 Computer Software**, **3.2 Hazardous Materials**, and adds **4.3 Distribution of Goods and Services** and **4.4 Project**. This conflicts with the revised July 2024 document, which lists **Handling Hazardous Substances**, **Self-Exploration and Career Development**, **Handling Waste Materials**, and only **4.3 Project**. The conflicting mirror may represent an earlier or differently edited version. Do not combine the two versions in one curriculum record. Use the revised 2024 version only when the implementation target is the current/rationalised design, and retain the conflict as provenance metadata until the live KICD PDF is directly retrievable.

## Implementation cautions

The subject is an integrated learning area combining business, computer, and technical studies. It should not be treated as a single workshop trade. The designs repeatedly connect practical work with communication, digital literacy, entrepreneurship, safety, financial literacy, and career awareness.

Do not infer a strand or sub-strand from a learning outcome, a textbook chapter, or a third-party scheme of work. Store the grade and design revision with every item. In particular, Grade 8 has a wording variance (“Ceramics” versus “Ceramic Materials”), and Grade 9 has a material version conflict. A data pipeline should preserve the source URL and exact source wording rather than normalising these variants without an audit trail.

The lesson counts in the designs are suggested allocations, not fixed universal timetables. The PDFs explicitly state that the number of lessons per sub-strand may be less or more depending on context. Practical activities also depend on safe facilities, equipment, digital devices, connectivity, and locally available materials. Safety-related strands should be implemented with appropriate supervision and protective equipment; the curriculum names safety as a learning focus but does not remove the need for school-level risk assessment.

The KICD index pages are the authoritative catalogue references. Because the direct KICD PDF downloads returned 404 responses during this research run, the exact grade-specific transcription should be rechecked against the current KICD-hosted PDFs before a high-stakes publication or automated curriculum migration. No learning outcomes have been invented or reconstructed here.
