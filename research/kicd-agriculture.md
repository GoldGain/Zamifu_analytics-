# KICD Agriculture for Kenyan Junior School (Grades 7–9)

## Scope and verification method

This note covers the Agriculture learning area for Kenyan Junior School Grades 7, 8 and 9. KICD grade-design landing pages were treated as the authoritative index. The pages identify Agriculture among the grade learning areas, but the current HTML extraction did not expose the PDF links. KICD’s PDF-viewer URLs and KICD-hosted filenames were recorded where discoverable. Because direct downloads of the apparent 2024 filenames returned HTTP 404 in this environment, grade-specific content was cross-checked against accessible reproductions of documents that identify KICD as publisher. Grade 9 strand/sub-strand content could not be verified against an accessible official KICD document and is therefore intentionally omitted from the structured result.

## Implementation cautions

1. Treat the KICD grade landing pages and the KICD-hosted PDF, not textbook notes or commercial summaries, as the source of truth.
2. Confirm edition/date and rationalisation status before implementation; the accessible Grade 7 and Grade 8 reproductions may not reflect a later KICD revision.
3. Preserve exact capitalization and wording of strand/sub-strand labels when importing curriculum metadata; do not normalise away distinctions such as “Conservation of Resources” versus “Conserving Agricultural Environment.”
4. Do not infer Grade 9 strands, sub-strands, lesson allocations, or learning outcomes from Grade 7/8 progression or from secondary notes.
5. The listed sub-strands are curriculum headings, not a complete transcription of specific learning outcomes. Outcomes should be extracted only from the verified official design edition.
6. Practical activities require local safety review, especially animal handling/slaughtering, sharp tools, chemicals/disinfectants, hot cooking equipment, and construction of waterers or garden structures.
7. Grade 8 design material indicates that schools may use limited or alternative spaces and locally available/recycled resources; implementation should adapt resources without changing the intended competency.
8. Where the official PDF is inaccessible, mark the record as provisional and schedule a source refresh rather than silently filling missing data.
