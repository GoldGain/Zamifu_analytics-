# KICD Social Studies Research Note: Kenyan Junior School Grades 7–9

## Scope and verification standard

This note covers exactly one subject: **Social Studies** in Kenyan Junior School Grades 7, 8, and 9. KICD pages and direct KICD PDF URLs were treated as authoritative. Because the KICD design pages rendered without their dynamic download listings in this environment, indexed curriculum copies and a curriculum-design mirror were used only to locate or cross-check the official material. No learning outcomes have been reconstructed or invented. The arrays in the machine-readable result should be treated as verified only where the strand/sub-strand names were visible in the curriculum-design text or in a cross-check that explicitly identifies the document as KICD-published.

## Verified grade-specific strands and sub-strands

### Grade 7

The 2024 revised/rationalized Grade 7 design is identified in the cross-check as first published in 2022 and revised in 2024. Its verified summary lists:

1. **Social Studies and Personal Development**: Self-Exploration; Social Entrepreneurial Opportunities.
2. **People and Relationships**: Human Origin; Early Civilisation; Slavery and Servitude; Developments in Medium of Trade; Diversity and Interpersonal Relationships; Peaceful Coexistence.
3. **Community Service Learning**: Community Service Learning Project.
4. **Natural and Historic Built Environments in Africa**: Historical Information; Historical Development of Agriculture; Maps and Map Work; Earth and the Solar System; Weather; Fieldwork.
5. **Political Development and Governance**: Political Development in Africa; The Constitution of Kenya; Human Rights; African Diasporas; Citizenship and Globalisation.

The KICD-indexed direct PDF URL is retained above. The official KICD page and direct PDF were discoverable, but the PDF download was blocked by the sandbox's web response, so the exact sub-strand list was cross-checked against the KICD-identified revised design copy and a secondary summary. This is a provenance caveat, not a claim that the KICD design is absent.

### Grade 8

The Grade 8 design's summary table visibly lists:

2. **Community Service Learning**: Community Service-Learning Project.

The Grade 9 revised design's summary table visibly lists:

1. **Social Studies and Career Development**: Pathway Choices; Pre-career Support Systems.
2. **Community Service-Learning**: Community Service-Learning Project.
3. **People and Relationships**: Socio-economic practices of early humans; Indigenous knowledge systems in African Societies; Poverty Reduction; Population Structure; Peaceful Conflict Resolution; Healthy Relationships.

A formatting inconsistency was observed in the extracted Grade 9 table of contents: it displayed strands 1–4 while the summary table and detailed section displayed strand 5. Because the summary table explicitly included strand 5.0 and its four sub-strands, it is reported here, but implementers should confirm against the current downloadable KICD file before using the list as a controlled curriculum master.

## Implementation cautions

1. **Use the current KICD revision, not an undated notes page.** Grade 7 is identified as first published in 2022 and revised in 2024; Grade 8 and Grade 9 copies also carry revised-design metadata in the cross-check material. Version and revision year should be stored with any curriculum import.
2. **Do not merge similarly named strands across grades.** Grade 7 uses “Social Studies and Personal Development,” Grade 8 uses “Social Studies and Personal Management,” and Grade 9 uses “Social Studies and Career Development.” These are grade-specific names.
3. **Preserve grade-specific ordering and punctuation where possible.** For example, “Natural and Historic Built Environments in Africa” is a Grade 7 title, while Grade 8 and Grade 9 omit “in Africa” in the strand title.
4. **Community Service Learning is integrated and project-based.** The designs include a CSL project and guidance/milestones; do not treat it as a conventional content-only unit or silently redistribute its hours without recording the local implementation decision.
5. **Suggested lesson numbers are not immutable timetables.** The Grade 8 and Grade 9 summaries explicitly say the suggested number may be more or less depending on context. They should not be interpreted as assessment weighting.
6. **Do not infer learning outcomes from sub-strand titles.** The present research verifies names only. Specific learning outcomes, inquiry questions, competencies, values, PCIs, resources, and assessment methods must be copied from the corresponding current KICD design rather than generated from the title.
7. **Check Grade 9 strand 5 in the live KICD PDF.** The summary and detailed text support it, but the extracted table of contents omitted it, likely a document-format or extraction issue. This is the clearest unresolved verification gap.
8. **Treat secondary sources as cross-checks.** Scribd, Teachers Palace, and MwalimuFocus are useful discovery/corroboration sources but should not replace the KICD page/PDF when a current official file is available.

## Bottom line

The grade-specific names above are sufficiently corroborated for a research dataset, with the explicit Grade 9 table-of-contents caveat and the Grade 7 direct-download access caveat. No unverified learning outcomes are supplied.
