# Mathematics for Kenyan Junior School Grades 7–9: KICD Research Note

## Scope and source policy
This note covers exactly one subject—**Mathematics**—for Kenyan Junior School Grades 7, 8, and 9. KICD index pages and KICD-hosted PDF URLs were treated as authoritative. Where the KICD HTML index was not machine-readable and the KICD PDF endpoint returned 404 during this check, an openly accessible curriculum-design copy was used only to cross-check headings; those items are marked accordingly and should not be treated as a substitute for a live KICD-hosted file.

## Implementation cautions
1. Preserve exact KICD spelling, numbering, punctuation, and capitalisation when a live official PDF is available; do not normalize names silently.
2. Treat a KICD index-page listing as evidence that a design exists, not as evidence of every strand or sub-strand name.
3. Do not infer Grade 8 or Grade 9 content by copying Grade 7 or by assuming progression from a textbook or scheme of work.
4. Keep Grade 9 arrays empty until the official file is accessible and headings can be transcribed directly.
5. The Grade 7 secondary copy labels the fifth strand “Data Handling and Probability” but its visible contents show “Data Handling” as the listed sub-strand; do not add “Probability” for Grade 7 without official confirmation.
6. Learning outcomes, suggested activities, lesson counts, and assessment guidance were intentionally not reproduced as verified facts because the official PDF endpoints were unavailable and the task prohibits invented content.
7. Re-run verification against the live KICD Grade Seven, Grade Eight, and Grade Nine Designs pages before production use, especially because KICD may replace or rationalize curriculum-design files.
