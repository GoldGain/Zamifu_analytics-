# Grade 8 curriculum and assessment-format audit

**Cut-off: 14 October 2026.** KICD curriculum content and KNEC assessment format are different authorities.

## Verified findings

- KICD's [Grade Eight Designs](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/grade-eight-designs/) index lists Agriculture, Arabic, Creative Arts, CRE, English, French, German, HRE, Indigenous Language, Integrated Science, IRE, Kiswahili, Mandarin, Mathematics, Pre-Technical Studies and Social Studies.
- KNEC's [2026 written-SBA guidance](https://www.knec.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Guidelines-and-schedule-of-Sep-2026-Grade-3-4-578-SBA.pdf) lists Grade 8 Mathematics, English, Kiswahili, KSL, Social Studies, CRE/IRE/HRE, Integrated Science, Agriculture, Creative Arts and Sports, and Pre-Technical Studies. Tools were accessible from **28 September 2026**, with scores due **23 October 2026**.
- That public circular does **not** specify national Paper 1/Paper 2 splits, paper codes, common marks, durations, parent-question counts or section totals for Grade 8. The authenticated CBA portal's actual tool and scoring guide control those details.
- KNEC's [2026 projects/performance guidance](https://www.knec.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Guidelines-and-schedule-for-Grades-378-and-SNE-Projects.pdf) lists Grade 8 Agriculture, Creative Arts and Sports, and Integrated Science. Tools were accessed **4 May 2026**; scores were due **31 July 2026**. These are project/performance windows, not timed national Paper 2 examinations.
- Teachers administer, score with supplied guidelines, upload outcomes, retain evidence and use locally available/improvised materials. The [2024 Grade 7/8 circular](https://www.knec.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Grade-7-and-8-SBAs.pdf) additionally describes PDF/digital delivery, colour-image handling and learner response materials. Its historical subject table must not override the 2026 schedule.

## Social Studies maps

The [official revised Grade 8 Social Studies design](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1yx30v28nVLKYSByRB9G2Omalh76-ZL6h/preview) includes **4.0 Natural and Historic Built Environments / 4.1 Map Reading and Interpretation**, allocated 12 lessons. Outcomes include marginal map information, scale conversion, distance and area calculations on topographical maps, and representation of drainage, relief and vegetation. These are curriculum skills, not a national map insert, fixed question position or prescribed paper marks.

Zamifu should include a complete original or supplied map when an item tests those skills. A country-outline map cannot be presented as a topographical sheet: it lacks surveyed contours, drainage, grids and exact scale. Unsupported maps must be rejected or replaced with an answerable supported task, never guessed.

## Implementation boundary

1. Default Grade 8 to **school-based CBE/SBA preparation**, with explicitly school-selected marks and timing.
2. Keep written assessments and projects/performance tasks distinct; do not impose Grade 9 KJSEA or legacy KCSE formats.
3. Treat marks, durations, national paper codes, item counts and rubrics as unknown until the actual portal tool is supplied.
4. Use source-verified Grade 8 curriculum trees and retain delivery/scoring/evidence metadata.
5. Make maps outcome-driven, complete and printable; do not falsely label a country map as topographical.
6. Grade 10 remains its own SBA regime; its [2026 written-test guidance](https://www.knec.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Guidelines-and-Schedule-for-Grade-10-Written-tests-2026.pdf) supplies no national Paper 1/Paper 2 split.

## Remaining uncertainties

The actual 2026 CBA portal tools were not accessed. Public circulars do not disclose their detailed marks, durations or rubrics. KSL is listed by KNEC but is absent from the visible KICD Grade Eight index; a school's authoritative KSL source must be configured before generation. No official Grade 8 national map insert or map-specific mark allocation was located.
