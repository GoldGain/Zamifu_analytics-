# Curriculum Audit: Religious Education (CRE/IRE), Kenyan CBC Junior School

**Audit scope.** This audit treats Kenyan Junior School Religious Education as the learning-area family containing the two requested streams: **Christian Religious Education (CRE)** and **Islamic Religious Education (IRE)**. It covers the current Grade 7, Grade 8 and Grade 9 curriculum designs and the KJSEA assessment/paper format. It does not treat HRE as part of the requested CRE/IRE scope.

**Status and source discipline.** The curriculum designs used for the strand/sub-strand transcription are the revised 2024 designs. The working PDF copies were downloaded from a non-official mirror because the KICD grade landing pages rendered without their PDF-link body in this environment. Each working PDF identifies **Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD)** as publisher and carries the grade, title and first-publication/revision information. The official KICD grade pages and direct KICD Grade 8 PDF endpoints were also checked. Mirror URLs are therefore evidence of the KICD-published document content, not independent authorities. The KNEC regulations and timetable below are direct official KNEC sources.

## Executive findings

The verified curriculum progression is not a single undifferentiated list. CRE and IRE are separate streams and their grade-specific strands differ substantially. IRE has seven recurring high-level strand families across Grades 7–9, while CRE has six strands in each grade, with progression from introductory CRE/creation/Bible content in Grade 7 to Jesus/church/Christian-living themes in Grades 8–9.

For the KJSEA, both **908 CRE** and **909 IRE** are currently specified by KNEC as **one written paper**, not Paper 1 plus Paper 2. Each is **100 marks** and **1 hour 30 minutes**, comprising **Section A: 20 multiple-choice questions (20 marks)** and **Section B: structured questions (80 marks)**. KNEC does not specify a separate practical paper, project paper, oral paper, or externally marked Paper 2 for either CRE or IRE in the regulations reviewed. Curriculum designs do contain classroom assessment methods such as oral/aural questions, written assignments, observation, portfolios and projects; those are formative/continuous assessment methods and should not be converted into an invented KJSEA practical component.

## Assessment and paper-format findings

1. **KJSEA CRE (code 908): one paper.** The official KNEC KJSEA Regulations state that Christian Religious Education is administered in one paper with two sections, marked out of **100**: Section A has **20 multiple-choice questions (20 marks)** and Section B has **structured questions (80 marks)**. The stated paper duration is **1 hour 30 minutes**.

2. **KJSEA IRE (code 909): one paper.** The same official regulations state that Islamic Religious Education is administered in one paper with two sections, marked out of **100**: Section A has **20 multiple-choice questions (20 marks)** and Section B has **structured questions (80 marks)**. The stated paper duration is **1 hour 30 minutes**.

3. **No KJSEA Paper 1/Paper 2 split was found for CRE or IRE.** The KNEC regulations list 908 and 909 as one-paper subjects. The official 2026 KJSEA timetable independently lists 908 CRE and 909 IRE as separate one-session papers, each scheduled for **1 hour 30 minutes**. The timetable does not show 908/1, 908/2, 909/1 or 909/2.

4. **Objective component.** The objective component is explicitly Section A: 20 multiple-choice questions for 20 marks. KNEC does not describe a separate OMR-only paper for CRE/IRE in the regulations excerpt; it does, however, issue general guidance about sample OMR sheets and the format of the KJSEA sample materials. Do not assume every CRE/IRE response is OMR-shaded; follow the official paper instructions and any current sample cover/rubric.

5. **Structured component.** Section B is explicitly described as structured questions worth 80 marks. The regulations do not specify a fixed number of Section B questions, a fixed five-marks-per-question allocation, or a compulsory essay section. Any local blueprint that hard-codes 16 five-mark questions is an implementation choice, not a KNEC-published requirement found in the audited sources.

6. **Practical/project component.** No separate CRE or IRE practical paper, oral paper, or project paper with external KJSEA marks and duration was found. The curriculum designs do list formative assessment methods (including oral/aural questions, written assignments, observation schedules, portfolios, rubrics and projects), and the CBC/CBA framework recognizes projects and performance evidence as competency-based tools. This should be modeled as classroom/formative evidence, not as an invented KJSEA Paper 2.

7. **Duration corroboration.** The KNEC Regulations provide the format, marks and duration; the official 2026 KJSEA timetable independently corroborates the 1 hour 30 minute duration for both 908 CRE and 909 IRE. The 2025 KJSEA sample-paper circular states that KNEC-developed sample papers are designed in the format to be used for KJSEA, but the circular is procedural and does not alter the CRE/IRE mark structure.

## Existing research and code audit

### `research/kicd-ire.md`

The existing note was not treated as authoritative. It correctly warned that the extractor had produced uncertain Grade 7 labels and that Grade 9 should not be filled by copying Grade 8. However, it is now stale against the revised 2024 documents: it leaves Grade 9 structured content empty even though the current Grade 9 design has a complete verified summary, and it presents only a partial Grade 7 transcription. The implementation should replace it with this audit or link to this audit as the current source of truth.

### `src/lib/kjsea-paper-formats.ts`

The current KJSEA format module maps `cre` and `ire` to non-two-paper subjects and gives each a 100-mark, 90-minute single-paper specification with 20 MCQs and 80 structured marks. That high-level mapping agrees with the audited KNEC regulations and timetable. The module also exposes placeholder `paper2` records marked “Not applicable”; callers correctly receive `null` for a requested Paper 2 because CRE/IRE are not in `TWO_PAPER_SUBJECTS`, but UI/API code should still prevent a misleading Paper 2 option from being displayed.

The blueprint helper hard-codes CRE/IRE Section B as **16 `case_study` items at five marks each**. KNEC’s official format only establishes an 80-mark structured section, not this exact question count or per-item allocation. This is the highest-priority paper-generation risk: preserve the 20-MCQ/80-structured totals, but make the structured-question count and mark distribution configurable or clearly label them as a local blueprint.

The source constant in the module still names the 2025 KJSEA timetable, while the audited current official timetable is the 2026 timetable. Update the source constant or store an explicit edition/version so a future format change cannot be silently missed. The generic format instruction also contains a Paper 2 “project/practical/structured” skeleton; it should not be presented for CRE/IRE.

In `src/lib/timetable-subject-code.ts`, the broad alias `religious education` maps to **CRE**. This is unsafe for a combined Religious Education label because a school offering IRE can be silently classified as CRE. Keep explicit CRE and IRE codes/aliases separate and require a stream-specific selection when the input is the umbrella label.

## Implementation risks and recommended controls

1. **CRE/IRE conflation:** Religious Education is a family containing distinct streams. Store a canonical stream (`CRE` or `IRE`) separately from the umbrella learning-area label; never infer CRE from “religious education.”
2. **Stale curriculum content:** The old IRE note is incomplete for Grade 7 and Grade 9. Version curriculum metadata by grade and edition (`revised 2024`) and retain source URLs.
3. **Unsupported paper split:** Do not expose Paper 1/Paper 2 for 908 or 909. A single-paper request should resolve to 100 marks and 90 minutes.
4. **Invented structured layout:** Do not claim that KNEC mandates 16 structured questions at five marks each. If the generator uses that layout, label it as Zamifu’s configurable blueprint and validate only the authoritative 80-mark total.
5. **Practical confusion:** Classroom projects, portfolios, oral/aural tasks and observation are valid CBC/CBA evidence but are not evidence of a KJSEA CRE/IRE practical paper. Keep formative and summative schemas distinct.
6. **Edition drift:** The code references a 2025 timetable while the current audited timetable is 2026. Add an edition field, refresh the URL and regression-test the source-backed values.
7. **Source transport versus publisher:** The grade-design working copies are mirrors, although the PDFs identify KICD as publisher. Keep the KICD grade landing-page URLs and direct KICD PDF URLs in provenance, and re-download directly from KICD when its link body is available.
8. **Typography and exact labels:** Qur’an/Quran, Ulum/Ulumul, S.A.W., Al-Hujurat, Iddah, Tawbah and capitalization vary by source table. Store an exact source label plus a normalized search/display key rather than silently rewriting the official text.
9. **Assessment language:** “Structured questions” should not be automatically treated as essays or case studies. Preserve a distinct structured-question type and allow sub-parts/marks to be configured from the official paper or current KNEC sample.
