## Verified grade-specific facts

The only grade-specific facts verified directly from KICD pages in this run are that each Grade 7, Grade 8, and Grade 9 curriculum-design page contains a **Creative Arts** category and an embedded grade-specific document. The actual PDF text, including the table of contents and learning outcomes, could not be inspected because the linked Google Drive files did not permit direct download in the sandbox. Accordingly, no strand or sub-strand names are asserted.

## Implementation cautions

1. Treat the arrays of strands and sub-strands as empty until the corresponding official KICD PDF can be opened and checked page by page.
2. Do not populate these fields from Scribd, Studocu, TeachersPalace, Arena, blog posts, or textbook summaries; such sources may be useful for locating a document but are not sufficient evidence for exact curriculum nomenclature.
3. Do not merge “Creative Arts” with “Creative Arts and Sports” without confirming the title and scope of the applicable KICD design. The subject label on the three KICD pages is “Creative Arts,” while other KICD materials and secondary listings may use different or revised labels.
4. Preserve the exact grade-specific PDF/preview URL in application metadata so that a future verification pass can replace the empty arrays with the document’s exact strand and sub-strand spellings.
5. Learning outcomes, suggested learning experiences, assessment criteria, time allocation, and progression should likewise remain unentered until verified from the official grade-specific design.
6. The KICD pages or linked files may change over time; record an access date and re-check the embedded Drive ID before implementation.

## Gap statement

This is a deliberately conservative result. The authoritative landing pages were reachable and their Creative Arts embeds were identified, but the source PDFs themselves were inaccessible through the available download route. Since the task prohibits inventing curriculum structure, the grade arrays must remain empty rather than being filled with unverified names.
