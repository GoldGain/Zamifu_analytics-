# Curriculum Audit: Pre-Technical Studies (CBC Junior Secondary)

**Audit scope.** This audit covers exactly one Kenyan CBC Junior Secondary learning area: **Pre-Technical Studies**, Grades 7, 8 and 9. It verifies the current strand/sub-strand hierarchy, reviews the repository's existing research and implementation, and records the evidenced KJSEA assessment/paper structure. The audit distinguishes official primary sources from readable third-party copies of curriculum designs and flags unresolved source-access or wording issues.

**Audit position.** The current KICD Grade 7, Grade 8 and Grade 9 curriculum-design index pages each currently list Pre-Technical Studies and embed an official Google Drive preview. The embedded document metadata identifies the documents as **Pre-Technical Studies Grade 7/8/9 – July 2024 – Revised**. The Drive previews are the official catalogue-linked artefacts, but their files were not downloadable in this environment because Google Drive returned an owner-download-permission restriction. The strand/sub-strand tables below were therefore checked against the readable copies of the same July 2024 revised designs hosted by Kenyan education publishers/teacher-resource sites. These copies are cross-check evidence, not a substitute for rechecking the body of the KICD-hosted files before a high-stakes migration.

### Grade progression observation

The current designs show a coherent progression from foundations, communication, materials, tools/production and entrepreneurship in every grade. The content is not a narrow workshop-trade sequence: it includes safety, ICT/computer concepts, drawing, materials, tools, financial literacy, entrepreneurship, career awareness and project work. Any implementation that replaces this with only technical drawing, woodwork, metalwork or building-construction packs is not a faithful representation of the current rationalised design.

## 3. Current assessment and paper-format findings

### 3.1 Official KNEC structure

The current KNEC KJSEA Regulations identify Pre-Technical Studies as learning area code **912** and state that it is **administered in two papers**:

| Paper | Official code/name | Evidenced format | Marks | Duration/evidence |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Paper 1 | **912/1 Pre-technical Studies** | Two sections: **Section A: 30 multiple-choice questions (30 marks)**; **Section B: structured and essay questions (50 marks)** | **80** | **1 hour 40 minutes** in the KNEC Regulations and 2025/2026 KNEC timetables. |
| Paper 2 | **912/2 Pre-technical Studies Project** | A **project**, to be done within one month in Term 2 of Grade 9 | **40** | **Project duration: 1 month**. It is not described by KNEC as a timed practical examination. |

The two papers therefore total **120 marks**, not 100. Paper 1 has an objective component and a written constructed-response component. The regulations specifically call Section B “structured and essay questions”; they do not provide evidence for a separate practical paper within 912/1. Paper 2 is a project assessed as candidates undertake tasks, rather than a conventional timed objective/structured/practical question booklet.

### 3.2 Project-administration evidence

KNEC's official 2026 project guidelines state that KNEC uploads the projects to the CBA Portal; subject teachers assess candidates as they undertake the tasks and score using the provided guidelines; materials should come from the immediate school environment or be improvised where possible; schools must maintain a file of evidence on project progress; and **912/2 Pre-Technical Studies** is accessed **1 July 2026**, has a **one-month** duration, and has a score-upload deadline of **30 August 2026**. The official 2025 circular states the same project-start context and evidence-file requirement for the 2025 administration. This is operational evidence for a supervised project workflow, not evidence of a conventional practical paper with a fixed examination-room duration.

### 3.3 What is and is not evidenced

* **Paper 1/Paper 2:** Yes. KNEC explicitly specifies both: 912/1 and 912/2.
* **Objective component:** Yes. Paper 1 Section A has 30 multiple-choice questions worth 30 marks.
* **Structured component:** Yes. Paper 1 Section B has structured and essay questions worth 50 marks.
* **Essay component:** Yes, because KNEC explicitly includes “essay questions” in Section B. The regulations do not publish a further split between structured and essay marks.
* **Practical component:** No separate timed practical paper is evidenced for 912. Paper 2 is officially a **project**. It is reasonable to describe it as task/project-based or practical-in-activity, but it should not be relabelled `Practical Paper` without an official source saying so.
* **Marks:** 912/1 = 80; 912/2 = 40; combined assessment = 120.
* **Duration:** 912/1 = 1 hour 40 minutes; 912/2 = one month. A one-month project is a calendar/project window, not 43,200 examination minutes and should not be summed with Paper 1 to create a “combined timed duration.”
* **Grade 7 and Grade 8 paper format:** The cited KJSEA sources specify the Grade 9 national assessment. They do not establish that Grades 7 and 8 use these same 912 paper codes or national-paper durations. Treat Grades 7/8 assessment as school/CBA/formative/summative assessment unless a separate official circular or school-level policy is supplied.

## 4. Audit of existing repository research and code

### Existing research

`research/kicd-pre-technical.md` is directionally useful: it identifies the five-strand structure, records the Grade 8 ceramics wording variance, records the conflicting older/different Grade 9 mirror, and already flags that the KICD PDF endpoints were not directly retrievable. This audit confirms the revised July 2024 hierarchy in that file. The new evidence that should be preserved is the current KICD index's embedded official Drive IDs and document titles:

* Grade 7 official Drive preview: `1vBIF-5Z0-hpYxr2YWJIrg1ATzO9f4YRH` — title `Pre-Technical Studies Grade 7 - July 2024 - Revised.pdf`.
* Grade 8 official Drive preview: `1Cvh2JSBlrHU4z4FKt7uVMgTMXEWq9jdu` — title `Pre-Technical Studies Grade 8 - July 2024 - Revised.pdf`.
* Grade 9 official Drive preview: `1sHIe4eN9oE8ojrjBPojqHj7YIdYdFWzP` — title `Pre-Technical Studies Grade 9 - July 2024 - Revised.pdf`.

The existing research should continue to mark the third-party copies as cross-check evidence and should not claim that the full official PDF body was directly downloaded in this environment.

### Current code and data findings

1. **Generic, non-grade-specific in-memory knowledge is wrong for this subject.** `src/lib/kicd-knowledge.ts` (`JUNIOR_STRAND_PACKS['Pre-Technical Studies']`, around lines 481–508) contains only two generic packs—`Technical Drawing and Design` and `Materials and Tools`—with generic sub-strands such as `Drawing Practice` and `Workshop Practice`. It does not represent any of the verified Grade 7/8/9 five-strand hierarchies.

2. **The repository's knowledge-chunk migration contains unsupported or stale trade hierarchies.** `supabase/migrations/20260725_populate_curriculum_data.sql` labels Grade 7/8/9 Pre-Technical rows as KICD curriculum data but uses entries such as Agriculture, Building Construction, Advanced CAD, Woodwork, Metalwork, Electrical and Mechanical Systems. Those rows do not match the July 2024 revised summary tables audited above. They should not remain approved curriculum scope without explicit provenance and replacement.

3. **The KJSEA paper metadata is substantially aligned on marks and Paper 1 structure.** `src/lib/kjsea-paper-formats.ts` defines Pre-Technical Paper 1 as code 912/1, 80 marks, 100 minutes, 30 MCQs plus a 50-mark structured section; it defines Paper 2 as 912/2, 40 marks and a one-month project. This matches the KNEC regulations/timetables at the high-level format level.

4. **Paper 2 is represented as `duration_minutes: 43200`.** That is mathematically one month at 30 days × 24 hours × 60 minutes, but it misrepresents the KNEC project window as an examination duration. It also creates a bad combined-paper representation: the `single`/combined spec adds 100 timed minutes to 43,200 project minutes. The data model should represent `duration_type: 'timed_exam' | 'project_window'`, with `duration_days: 30` or `project_duration_label: '1 month'` for 912/2, and must not sum the two as one timed duration.

5. **The Paper 2 blueprint is too paper-like for the official project.** `paperSections()` creates one 40-mark `case_study` section for 912/2. KNEC describes a CBA Portal project with teacher scoring guidelines and an evidence file. A printable generated question may be useful as a school practice task, but it is not the official project tool, rubric, confidential portal task or evidence-management workflow. The implementation must label any generated artefact as a practice/project brief unless it has the official task and scoring guidance.

6. **The UI exposes a combined “Paper 1 + Paper 2” option.** `ExamGenerator.tsx` displays marks and minutes for a combined variant. For Pre-Technical this can display a nonsensical very large minute total because the project window is stored as minutes. The UI should show `80 marks · 1 h 40 min` for Paper 1 and `40 marks · 1 month project window` for Paper 2, and avoid presenting a combined timed duration.

7. **The paper-format regression test does not cover Pre-Technical.** `scripts/test-kjsea-paper-formats.ts` tests English, Kiswahili and Integrated Science only. It should add explicit 912/1 and 912/2 assertions, including 80/40 marks, 100-minute Paper 1, one-month project-window semantics, and prevention of a 120-mark combined paper being treated as a single timed exam.

8. **Curriculum loading and paper defaults need strict grade/provenance handling.** `src/lib/kicd-defaults.ts` can select a first matching row when a grade-specific row is unavailable, while the exam UI and generic knowledge paths are not enough to guarantee the selected Grade 7, Grade 8 or Grade 9 hierarchy. For this subject, every strand/sub-strand row should carry grade, design revision, source URL, source wording and an approval state. Never fall back from one grade's Pre-Technical hierarchy to another grade's hierarchy.

9. **The application must separate curriculum project strands from KJSEA project administration.** Grade 9 has a curriculum sub-strand `4.3 Project`, while KNEC has a separately coded 912/2 project. They may be related in content, but the application should retain both provenance paths and not assume that the 20 suggested curriculum lessons are identical to the one-month KJSEA project window.

## 5. Implementation risks and required controls

| Risk | Consequence | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Stale generic/trade curriculum packs remain approved | Questions can be generated from topics not in the current revised design | Replace or quarantine the unsupported migration rows; seed exact grade-specific hierarchy with source metadata. |
| Grade 8 `Ceramics`/`Ceramic Materials` is normalized without an alias | Existing questions or teacher selections may fail to match the canonical label | Store canonical `Ceramic Materials` plus source alias `Ceramics`; normalize only at search/display boundaries. |
| Grade 9 older mirror is merged with revised 2024 design | Duplicate or contradictory sub-strands enter generation | Use the revised July 2024 hierarchy only, retain older mirror as a conflict/provenance record. |
| KICD official PDF body is not rechecked after Drive permissions change | A later revision could be missed | Periodically re-fetch the current KICD index and Drive metadata; require human verification of the PDF body before high-stakes migration. |
| One-month project represented as minutes | UI and combined-paper logic report a false exam duration | Add project-window fields and prohibit arithmetic addition of project windows and timed-paper durations. |
| Project generated as ordinary 40-mark case study | Schools may mistake practice content for the confidential KNEC task/rubric | Label generated project briefs as practice; keep official task/rubric/CBA upload as a separate workflow. |
| Paper 1 structure allows arbitrary question-type choices | Output may not preserve 30 MCQ + 50 structured/essay architecture | Lock or validate 912/1 blueprint and total marks; preserve objective versus constructed-response sections. |
| No national Grade 7/8 paper evidence | Grade 9 KJSEA timing may be incorrectly applied to lower grades | Keep Grade 7/8 classroom/CBA assessment metadata separate from Grade 9 KJSEA 912 metadata. |
| Practical work in curriculum tables is confused with a national practical paper | Incorrect paper labels and unsupported marks/durations | Use `project`/`task-based` language for 912/2 unless KNEC publishes a separate practical-paper designation. |
| Safety/project requirements are omitted | Unsafe or non-compliant school activities | Carry forward local-materials/improvisation, supervision, PPE/risk assessment, teacher scoring and evidence-file requirements. |
