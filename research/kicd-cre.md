# Christian Religious Education (CRE), Kenyan Junior School Grades 7–9

## Scope and source discipline

This note records only material that could be tied to the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) or to an official KICD document URL indexed by search. The KICD grade-design landing pages were consulted first, but their curriculum-item content was not exposed in the extracted HTML. Search indexing identified official KICD CRE PDF URLs for Grade 7 and Grade 8. Direct retrieval in this environment returned HTTP 404 for the indexed PDF paths, so the exact contents of those official files were not treated as independently verified from the live KICD file. A secondary mirror of the Grade 8 design was used only to cross-check labels that appear in the KICD-indexed document; it is not an authoritative source.

## Grades 7 and 9 verification gaps

**Grade 7:** The KICD Grade 7 landing page and the indexed official PDF URL were identified, but the official PDF was not retrievable in this run and no exact Grade 7 strand/sub-strand table was accepted from a secondary source. Consequently, the structured Grade 7 arrays should remain empty.

**Grade 9:** The KICD Grade 9 landing page was identified, but no retrievable official CRE design file with an exact grade-specific table was verified. Consequently, the structured Grade 9 arrays should remain empty. Do not backfill Grade 9 from generic CBC, a textbook, an examination guide, or an unverified mirror.

## Implementation cautions

1. Treat the KICD landing pages as authoritative discovery pages, but preserve the exact PDF URL and retrieval status; a search result or viewer URL is not itself evidence that the current PDF bytes are available.
2. Keep Grade 7 and Grade 9 arrays empty until an official KICD design or an official KICD-hosted copy can be opened and checked page by page.
3. For Grade 8, distinguish “cross-checked against a mirror of the KICD-indexed design” from “verified from the live official PDF.” Avoid silently presenting the mirror as KICD-authored evidence.
4. Preserve numbering, capitalization, and punctuation from the curriculum table. In particular, do not collapse “Work: Talents, Gifts and Abilities,” “The Lost Sheep,” or “Healing of Blind Bartimaeus” into invented alternative labels.
5. Do not infer progression across grades. Similar-looking strands in another grade, a rationalised design, or a textbook must not be copied into a missing grade.
6. CRE is a religious education learning area; implementation should follow the official design’s learning outcomes, suggested activities, assessment guidance, inclusion requirements, and school policy rather than treating the strand names alone as a complete syllabus.
7. Re-check KICD for revised/rationalised designs before production use, because the indexed 2022/2023 URLs may have been migrated or replaced.
