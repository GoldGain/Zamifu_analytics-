# KJSEA Sample-Paper Format Research Report

**Research basis.** This report records the January 2025 KJSEA sample-paper structures reviewed for the six priority papers. The accessible papers were KNEC-branded public mirrors linked by Kenyan teacher-resource sites; KNEC's own circular states that sample papers and rubrics are also available through the authenticated CBA Portal. Sample-paper question text is intentionally not reproduced here.

## Cross-paper source policy

The authoritative baseline was the KNEC regulations, timetable, and sample-paper access circular. The paper-specific structure was taken from the inspected KNEC-branded sample where available, cross-checked against at least one Kenyan teacher/revision host and ExamWise's public format pages. ExamWise was treated as a product-format comparison only; its generic 100-mark KJSEA examples conflict with the official 50-, 70-, and 30-mark papers and were not used as the source of truth.

## Paper findings

### English Paper 1 — 901/1

The header reads **KJSEA 901/1, English (Reading Comprehension, Oral Skills and Grammar), Paper 1, Sample Paper, January 2025**, with **1 hour 40 minutes** and 50 marks. The paper is 50 continuous objective questions numbered 1–50, with four options A–D and one selected response per item. Questions 1–20 contain four prose comprehension groups: 1–6, 7–11, 12–16, and 17–20. Questions 21–25 test oral skills through dialogue completion and situational responses. Questions 26–35 form one ten-blank cloze passage. Questions 36–50 cover grammar and language use, including completion, punctuation, transformation, combination, meaning, and idiom items. There are no open-response sub-parts and no instructional charts or diagrams; prose, dialogue, and cloze text are the stimuli.

The sample uses a separate answer sheet and an A–D box-selection convention. The later KNEC circular describes the reviewed 2025 OMR design as shaded/fill-circle responses, so the generator now describes an answer-sheet/one-choice convention without copying a particular physical marking instruction. No public official paper-specific marking scheme was verified; the implementation therefore requires a keyed option and does not invent alternative-answer rules.

**Implementation difference.** The previous simplified model listed the same four broad components but did not enforce the exact Q1–50 block ranges or prohibit open sub-parts. The new blueprint enforces the observed numbering and question-type sequence.

### English Paper 2 — 901/2

The header reads **KJSEA 901/2, English (Composition and Literary Analysis), Paper 2, Sample Paper, January 2025**, with **1 hour 45 minutes** and 50 marks. It has two sections and five compulsory numbered tasks. Section A is Question 1 composition for 15 marks. Section B contains Question 2 oral literature for 10 marks, Question 3 poetry for 5 marks, Question 4 play for 10 marks, and Question 5 novella for 10 marks. The inspected sample uses prose literary extracts, a lineated poem, play speaker labels/stage directions, and novella prose. Sub-parts use lower-case letters and nested Roman items; marks are printed parenthetically.

The regulations describe the same 15/10/10/10/5 allocation but place the literary components conceptually in a different order. The sample's numbered task order—oral literature, poetry, play, novella—is used by the generator because the addendum requires the actual sample structure. No public official paper-specific marking scheme was verified; the generator requires an explicit model answer and marking rationale without claiming unverified KNEC alternative-answer rules.

**Implementation difference.** The earlier model had the right total allocation but placed poetry after the other literary tasks and described Paper 2 generically. The new blueprint follows the sample's Q1–Q5 order and its literary stimulus conventions.

### Kiswahili Paper 1 — 902/1

The header reads **KJSEA 902/1, Kiswahili Lugha, Karatasi ya 1, Sample Paper, Januari 2025**, with **Saa 1 dakika 40** and 50 questions. It is an all-objective paper numbered Q1–Q50 with four options A–D. Q1–Q20 are four ufahamu blocks; Q21–Q25 are a scripted kusikiliza na kuzungumza block; Q26–Q35 are a ten-blank cloze passage; and Q36–Q50 are standalone sarufi/language-use items. Q18–Q20 include a functional data-table reading task. The January booklet uses task directions rather than formal Section A/B labels. No graphs, maps, or diagrams were observed.

The sample implies one mark per item and uses a separate answer sheet. The later KNEC circular's shaded OMR guidance is treated as the current administration convention. No public official answer key was verified.

**Implementation difference.** The earlier model represented the cloze test as an undifferentiated component and called grammar 15 marks without preserving the exact Q1–Q50 sequence. The new blueprint enforces the four observed blocks and the data-table possibility.

### Kiswahili Paper 2 — 902/2

The header reads **KJSEA 902/2, Kiswahili (Insha na Utangulizi wa Fasihi), Karatasi ya 2, Sample Paper, Januari 2025**, with **Saa 1 dakika 45** and 50 marks. Section A is Insha, Question 1, for 15 marks; the recommended composition length is 300–350 words. Section B contains Question 2 Fasihi Simulizi for 10 marks, Question 3 Novela for 10 marks, Question 4 Tamthilia for 10 marks, and Question 5 Ushairi for 5 marks. The observed sub-part totals are 2+2+1+2+2+1 for Fasihi Simulizi; five 2-mark sub-parts for Novela; 2+4+2+2 for Tamthilia; and 2+1+2 for Ushairi. Marks are printed using Kiswahili alama notation.

The sample is text-only: prose, drama dialogue/stage directions, and poetry. No public official marking scheme was verified; the implementation records that exact accepted alternatives and partial-credit rules remain unavailable.

**Implementation difference.** The earlier model had the correct five component totals but did not preserve the explicit question numbering, word-length expectation, Kiswahili mark notation, or sub-part mark distributions. These are now part of the prompt guidance and blueprint titles.

### Integrated Science Paper 1 — 905/1

The header reads **KJSEA 905/1, Integrated Science (Theory), Paper 1, January 2025**, with **1 hour 40 minutes**. The inspected sample has 70 marks and 18 printed pages. Section A contains Q1–Q30 multiple-choice questions worth 30 marks, using A–D options and a separate answer sheet. Section B contains written structured questions Q31–Q40 worth 40 marks: Task 1/Q31 is 3 marks; Task 2/Q32–34 is 13 marks; Task 3/Q35–38 is 16 marks; and Task 4/Q39–40 is 8 marks. The paper uses explicit parenthetical sub-part marks and lower-case/Roman sub-parts.

The sample integrates biology, chemistry, and physics contexts. Observed visuals include hazard symbols, particle arrangements, organs, magnets, circuits, tables, separation setups, flow charts, and a displacement-versus-distance waveform. Visuals are shared stimuli tied to adjacent questions and should be generated only when required by the selected content.

**Implementation difference.** The earlier model had only two coarse sections—30 MCQs and four ten-mark structured questions—which did not match the actual task groups or 3/13/16/8 allocation. The new blueprint preserves the observed task groups and visual conventions.

### Integrated Science Paper 2 — 905/2

The inspected January 2025 sample is a **four-page written practical-skills paper** with **30 marks**, **1 hour 30 minutes**, and **two compulsory questions**. Question 1 is chemistry/indicator work for 20 marks, using an observation/conclusion table, solution replacement, science-skills, and safety prompts. Question 2 is physics/measurement for 10 marks, using ruler and wooden-block measurements, volume calculation, and SI-unit conversion. The paper has no formal Section A/B division and no MCQ block. The main visual is the Question 1 observation table; no graph or diagram was observed.

**Important caveat.** The current KNEC regulations describe a later three-question written 905/2 coding/format variation and a different duration/order. The January 2025 sample is used for sample-style generation because it is the actual inspected paper requested by this addendum, while the implementation note explicitly preserves the later-regulation conflict. No public official marking scheme was verified; apparatus answers, measured values, accepted alternatives, and partial-credit rules are not invented.

**Implementation difference.** The earlier model incorrectly generated three 10-mark Chemistry/Biology/Physics practical questions and used 60 minutes. The new sample-backed model generates two tasks with 20/10 marks and 90 minutes and warns the prompt not to invent the later three-question split.

## Code changes

The per-paper definitions, section components, source URLs, and sample-style prompt guidance are implemented in [`src/lib/kjsea-paper-formats.ts`](../src/lib/kjsea-paper-formats.ts). The Paper 1/Paper 2 subject gate and paper-focus law remain in [`src/lib/exam-construction.ts`](../src/lib/exam-construction.ts). The existing regression suite was updated for the evidence-backed Integrated Science Paper 2 duration.

The prompt builder receives format-only style guidance derived from the reports. It receives structural patterns, section names, numbering, mark allocations, and visual conventions—not copied question text, answer keys, or marking-scheme prose. This keeps the generated papers original while requiring them to resemble the inspected paper architecture.
