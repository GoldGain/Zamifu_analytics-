# KICD Kiswahili for Kenyan Junior School Grades 7–9

## Conclusion and source status

The Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) publishes separate Grade Seven, Grade Eight, and Grade Nine curriculum-design landing pages. Those pages identify **Kiswahili** as a learning area. The linked PDF files were not retrievable from the KICD WordPress media paths during this research session: each returned HTTP 404. The official landing pages nevertheless remain the authoritative source locations, and their subject labels were verified. The exact grade-specific names below were transcribed from matching Kiswahili curriculum-design PDFs distributed through a secondary index; the PDFs themselves state that they are published by the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development. This distinction matters: the content is a cross-check of official material, while the KICD landing pages are the primary source URLs.

## Verified grade strands

### Grade 7

The Grade 7 design uses the following 15 strands: **Usafi wa Kibinafsi; Lishe Bora; Uhuru wa Wanyama; Aina za Maliasili; Unyanyasaji wa Kijinsia; Usalama Shuleni; Kuhudumia Jamii Shuleni; Ulaguzi wa Binadamu; Matumizi ya Vifaa vya Kidijitali katika Mawasiliano; Kijithamini; Majukumu ya Watoto; Magonjwa Ambukizi; Utatuzi wa Migogoro; [strand 14 not independently recovered in the text extraction]; [strand 15 not independently recovered in the text extraction].** Because the final two Grade 7 strand headings could not be independently recovered from the available text extraction, they should not be treated as verified names in application data.

Verified sub-strand labels include: **Kusikiliza; Kusoma; Kuandika; Sarufi** as the recurring instructional sub-strand headings, with specific labels such as **Hadithi: Mighani**, **Kusoma kwa Mapana: Matini ya kujichagulia**, **Insha za Kubuni: Masimulizi**, and **Vihusishi: Vihusishi vya -a Unganifu na Vihusishi vya Sababu** appearing in the extracted design text. The available extraction did not support a complete, reliable Grade 7 sub-strand inventory; do not infer missing items from Grade 8 or Grade 9.

## Implementation cautions

1. Treat the three KICD grade pages as the authoritative discovery locations and retain the exact URLs in metadata.
2. Do not merge strands across grades. Several themes recur with different wording, and Grade 7, Grade 8, and Grade 9 are separate designs.
3. Preserve Kiswahili diacritics, capitalization, slash notation, hyphens, and punctuation when a source provides them. Do not silently translate strand or sub-strand names.
4. The KICD media URLs returned 404 in this session. A later import should re-check the landing pages and official PDF links before replacing the cross-check text.
5. Where a sub-strand line was truncated by PDF text extraction, leave it out rather than reconstructing it from context. In particular, do not use Grade 8 or Grade 9 wording to fill Grade 7 gaps.
6. The KICD design documents include learning outcomes, learning experiences, inquiry questions, values, competencies, and assessment guidance. This note reports names only; it does not infer or paraphrase outcomes.
