# Kenyan CBE Grade 9 curriculum and assessment-format audit

**Cut-off:** 14 October 2026. **Scope:** Age-based Kenyan CBE Grade 9 (KJSEA), with a narrow Grade 10 check so Grade 10 is not given an unsupported KCSE-style format.

## Authority split and subject scope

**KICD is the curriculum authority; KNEC is the national-assessment authority.** KICD’s current Grade Nine Designs page is the content index. It lists 16 Grade 9 curriculum-design areas: Agriculture, Arabic, Creative Arts, CRE, English, French, German, HRE, Indigenous Language, Integrated Science, IRE, Kiswahili, Mandarin, Mathematics, Pre-Technical Studies and Social Studies. The linked design documents are the source for strands, sub-strands, learning outcomes and classroom content—not for national paper codes or marks. [5]

The current KNEC KJSEA Regulations narrow the **national Grade 9 summative assessment** to 12 subjects and 19 papers: English, Kiswahili, Mathematics, Kenyan Sign Language (KSL), Integrated Science, Agriculture, Social Studies, CRE, IRE, HRE, Creative Arts & Sports and Pre-technical Studies. [1] Therefore, do not create KJSEA national papers for Arabic, French, German, Indigenous Language or Mandarin merely because KICD lists their curriculum designs. Conversely, KSL is in the KNEC KJSEA list and needs a format entry even though it is not shown in the KICD Grade Nine page index.

KJSEA covers work from Grades 7, 8 and 9. Its national written assessment uses both MCQs and structured questions: MCQs are shaded on personalised OMR forms and structured responses use personalised answer sheets. [4]

## Verified 2026 KJSEA national paper formats

The following are the regular candidate marks, durations and components in the 2026 KNEC Regulations. The 2026 timetable confirms the regular written-paper durations and dates. [1] [2] Exact parent-question counts, numbering, equal mark-per-question assumptions and fixed internal allocations are **not** national rules unless stated below.

- **English (901, two papers):** 901/1 Language — **50 marks, 1 h 40 min**: Listening & Speaking 5, Cloze Test 10, Reading Comprehension (four passages) 20, Grammar 15. 901/2 English Composition & Literary Analysis — **50 marks, 1 h 45 min**: Composition 15; Oral Literature 10; Novella/Short Story 10; Play 10; Poetry 5. [1]
- **Kiswahili (902, two papers):** 902/1 Kiswahili Lugha — **50 marks, 1 h 40 min**: Ufahamu wa Kusoma (four passages) 20, Kusikiliza na Kuzungumza 5, Sarufi 25. 902/2 Insha na Utangulizi wa Fasihi — **50 marks, 1 h 45 min**: Insha 15; Fasihi Simulizi 10; Novela 10; Tamthilia 10; Ushairi 5. [1]
- **Mathematics (903, one paper):** **100 marks, 2 h**. Section A is 20 MCQs/20 marks; Section B is structured and essay questions/80 marks. KNEC does not state a fixed Section B parent-question count or a 5-mark-per-question split. [1]
- **Kenyan Sign Language (904, two papers):** 904/1 Structures and Function—Receptive Skills, **60 marks, 1 h 40 min**: Observe and Sign 15; View and Interpret 30; Grammar 15. 904/2 Language Use and Content—Expressive Skills, **40 marks, 1 h 40 min**: written story based on a series of graphics 20; essay based on thematic areas and PCIs 20. [1]
- **Integrated Science (905, two papers):** 905/1 **70 marks, 1 h 40 min**: Section A 30 MCQs/30; Section B structured and essay questions/40. 905/2 **30 marks, 1 h**: a written practical-skills paper with three questions—Chemistry, Biology and Physics. The 2026 Regulations require the three questions but do not allocate their individual marks. [1] [2]
- **Agriculture (906, two components):** 906/1 **70 marks, 1 h 40 min**: Section A 30 MCQs/30; Section B structured and essay questions/40. 906/2 is a **30-mark practical/project component**. The Regulations table prints “2 hours 30 minutes” for 906/2, but the 2026 timetable and the 2026 project guidance operationalise it as a one-month project window beginning 1 July. Treat this as a source conflict, not as permission to schedule a 150-minute paper. [1] [2] [3]
- **Social Studies (907, one paper):** **100 marks, 1 h 30 min**: Section A 20 MCQs/20; Section B structured and essay questions/80. No national parent-question count, item-number placement or internal Section B allocation is specified in the current Regulations. [1]
- **CRE (908), IRE (909) and HRE (910), each one paper:** **100 marks, 1 h 30 min**: Section A 20 MCQs/20; Section B structured questions/80. The Regulations do not prescribe 16 five-mark questions. [1]
- **Creative Arts & Sports (911, two components):** 911/1 integrated project—**100 marks, three months**, comprising Performing Arts, Visual Arts and Sports. 911/2 written paper—**100 marks, 1 h 40 min**: Section A 40 MCQs/40; Section B structured questions/60. [1] [3]
- **Pre-technical Studies (912, two components):** 912/1 **80 marks, 1 h 40 min**: Section A 30 MCQs/30; Section B structured and essay questions/50. 912/2 project—**40 marks, one month in Term 2 of Grade 9**. [1] [3]

The 2026 timetable places the regular written papers on 26–30 October 2026: English on 26 October; Kiswahili and KSL on 27 October; Mathematics and Agriculture 906/1 on 28 October; Integrated Science 905/1, Social Studies and Creative Arts & Sports 911/2 on 29 October; Pre-technical 912/1, CRE/IRE/HRE and Integrated Science 905/2 on 30 October. It also says no extra time is allowed and that reading time is included unless the paper says otherwise. [2]

## Projects, practicals and school-based assessment

The 2026 KNEC project circular says KNEC uploads project tools to the CBA portal; subject teachers assess candidates as they work and score with the supplied guidelines. Schools must maintain evidence files and upload all project scores by **30 August 2026**. Late upload attracts **Ksh 500 per candidate**. [3]

Operational 2026 windows are: **911/1** tools accessed 11 May, duration three months, scores due 30 August; **912/2** and **906/2** tools accessed 1 July, duration one month, scores due 30 August. [3] These are project windows, not minutes to render as a timed written paper. Integrated Science 905/2 is different: it is a one-hour written practical-skills paper on 30 October. [1] [2]

For SBAs, KNEC’s current user guide says KNEC sets the dates and uploads tools to the CBA portal; tools may be administered on paper or digitally; the headteacher/principal controls secure access; subject teachers administer, score and upload; evidence is kept in assessment records; feedback is reported to learners and parents through the School Year Report; and every learner undertakes SBA at each grade. SBAs contribute to reporting on KJSEA achievement. [4]

KJSEA eligibility requires a KPSEA result and Grade 7 and 8 SBA scores in all learning areas. [1] The user guide’s explicit 60% SBA/40% summative weighting is stated for KILEA/KPLEA, not KJSEA. Do not reuse that 60/40 percentage for Grade 9 without a new KNEC result/placement rule. [4]

## Maps, samples and what is not specified

The current 2026 timetable’s candidate instruction is conditional: **if** a question indicates that a map or other material should have been provided and it is missing, the candidate must tell the Supervisor. This confirms handling of supplied materials; it does **not** prescribe a Social Studies map count, map type, question number or universal map requirement. [2] The KJSEA Regulations only give Social Studies Section A/Section B aggregates. [1]

For Zamifu, make maps an optional, content-driven Social Studies stimulus whose inclusion is controlled by the selected KICD learning outcome and the current KNEC sample/rubric, not by a hard “at least one map” rule. If a generated item requires a map, store the map asset/material requirement explicitly in the item metadata and render a supplied map/other material notice. The public KNEC sample-paper circular says the samples, OMR sheets and rubrics are accessed inside the authenticated Grade 9 CBA portal; the 2025 samples were designed to familiarise schools with the then-current question-paper format. [6] Do not silently promote 2025 sample numbering or 2025 item ranges to 2026 national mandates.

## Grade 10 boundary

The first senior CBE cohort must remain separate from KJSEA. The official 2026 Grade 10 document is explicitly titled **Grade 10 School Based Assessment (SBA) Written Tests**. KNEC uploads tools to the CBA portal; principals download them; subject teachers administer, score and upload outcomes. The schedule is 5–23 October 2026 and lists STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports subject groups. [7]

That source does **not** publish a national Grade 10 Paper 1/Paper 2 split, paper codes, common marks, or common durations. Therefore, as of this cut-off, there is no source basis to impose legacy KCSE Paper 1/Paper 2 on Grade 10 or to reuse Grade 9 KJSEA structures for Grades 7, 8 or 10.

## Audit of `src/lib/kjsea-paper-formats.ts`

The declared Grade 9 metadata is broadly aligned with the 2026 KNEC component totals for the 11 subjects it currently models, but it needs these corrections before being treated as authoritative:

1. Add **KSL (904/1 and 904/2)** and include it in the two-paper subject set; the current union omits an official KJSEA subject and makes 18 rather than 19 papers.
2. Keep 2026 regulation components and aggregates separate from historical sample style. Do not expose English/Kiswahili five-task numbering, Math/CRE/IRE/HRE 16×5 assumptions, Social Studies Q21–Q34 placement, Integrated Science Paper 1’s 2025 ten-question split, Agriculture’s three×10 practical split, or fixed 20/20 Pre-technical and 25×4 Creative Arts project criteria as national mandates. Current KNEC regulations do not state those internal allocations. [1] [6]
3. Treat 905/2 as three practical-skills questions but leave the internal mark split configurable/unknown; the current code’s 10/10/10 balance is only a provisional blueprint. [1]
4. Treat 906/2, 912/2 and 911/1 as project-window components with marks, access date, duration window and upload deadline—not timed papers. Preserve the 906/2 Regulations-versus-timetable duration conflict in source metadata. [1] [2] [3]
5. Remove the hard Social Studies “at least one map” requirement and historical Q21 map placement. Use an optional `required_materials` field when a current official task calls for a map. [1] [2]
6. Add a separate KICD content-source field and a KNEC format-source field. KICD design content should drive strand/outcome coverage; KNEC regulations/timetable should drive paper code, marks, duration and component type. Do not use third-party sample sites as authority.
7. Keep Grade 10 in a separate SBA configuration with no national paper split until KNEC publishes one. Do not apply KJSEA metadata to Grades 7, 8 or 10.

## Material unknowns requiring a source refresh

- KNEC’s authenticated 2026 Grade 9 portal contains the operational sample papers, rubrics, OMR and cover pages, but the public circular does not expose their full content. Exact 2026 parent-question counts, numbering, mark-per-subpart patterns and task wording therefore remain unknown.
- KNEC’s public sources do not state an internal mark split for Integrated Science 905/2’s three questions, 906/2 practical/project tasks, or the project criterion breakdowns for 911/1 and 912/2.
- The 906/2 Regulations entry says 2 h 30 min while the 2026 timetable and project circular say one month. Use the project schedule operationally and flag the inconsistency for manual review.
- No public 2026 national Grade 10 Paper 1/Paper 2 specification was found; the current official evidence is an SBA written-test schedule only.
